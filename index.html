📝 Workplace Email Writing Tips
💼

Key Principles for Professional Emails

Noun Usage: Use possessive forms (-'s or -s') correctly to show ownership. Match noun number (singular/plural) to the context and verbs.
Verb Consistency: Follow subject-verb agreement (e.g., third-person singular verbs need -s/-es). Use base verbs after "to" (infinitives) and modal verbs (would, should).
Preposition Collocations: Learn fixed phrases like "owing to" instead of mixing prepositions randomly.
Article Rules: Omit "the" before proper nouns like festivals (Easter, Christmas). Use articles appropriately for common nouns.
Pronoun Completeness: Add necessary pronouns (e.g., "it" in "appreciate it if") to avoid sentence fragments.
Spelling & Capitalisation: Double-check spelling (e.g., "without" not "with out") and capitalise proper nouns, sentence starters, and formal titles.
Conciseness: Keep sentences clear and direct. Avoid redundant words in professional communication.
Formality: Maintain a polite tone with phrases like "we would appreciate it if" instead of informal language.
